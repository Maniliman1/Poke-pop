# Poké Pop Defense

A free fan-made tower defense game for the browser, in the style of Bloons TD with Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team sprites, dungeons and story.

**Play:** open the page (`index.html`) in any modern browser, desktop or phone. No install, no account. Keep the `packs` folder next to `index.html`: the game downloads its art from there a little at a time, as it is needed.

## How to play
- Pick a Pokémon from your Team panel and place it beside the path. Press Start wave.
- Wild Pokémon walk the path toward your base. Knock them out to earn money (₽) for placing and upgrading Pokémon.
- Upgrade Range and Attack to teach them stronger moves. Each dungeon is a fresh run.
- Every wave you survive pays coins (🪙), with a bonus for clearing the dungeon. Coins buy friend areas and, after your first full clear, evolutions.
- Clear the 20 story dungeons. Some defeats are normal: try damage-heavy teams or teams that slow and stun.
