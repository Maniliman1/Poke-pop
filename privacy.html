Privacy

Poke-TD (poke-td.com) is a free, unofficial fan-made game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, Creatures, Game Freak or Spike Chunsoft.

Without an account

You do not need an account to play. Your progress is saved only in your own browser (local storage) and never leaves your device. The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

With an account

If you create an account, we store your email address, your password and the game save you choose to upload. Your password is kept in scrambled form by our provider, so nobody, including us, can read it.

Why

Only to let you log in, back up your progress, restore it on another device, and send you a password reset email if you ask for one. We do not send news or promotions.

Who handles it

Accounts and saves are stored by Supabase, a hosting service, on its servers. We never sell or share your data. The game is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy.

The game links to our Discord server and to an X profile. They are other websites with their own privacy rules. The game sends them nothing unless you click a link.

How long

Until you delete your account.

Your rights

You can delete your account and your cloud save at any time in the game: Settings → Account → Delete my account. You can also ask us to show, correct or delete your data. If you are in the EU or the UK, you can complain to your data protection authority.

Age

Accounts are for people aged 13 or older. If you are under 16 (or under the age of digital consent in your country), a parent or guardian must agree.

Using the game

The game is free and provided as it is, without any guarantee. Saves can be lost, for example if you clear your browser data, and we may change or stop the game or the accounts at any time. Please do not misuse the account service.
